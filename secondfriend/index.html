Our Mission

The hardest conversation is often the first one. We're here to make it easier.

Second Friend exists to give college students a private, comfortable way to take that first step and talk to a real human who's ready to listen.

Our Vision

A future where “I’m fine” doesn’t have to be the end of the conversation.

We envision a world where no student feels they have to struggle silently just because asking for help feels harder than pretending everything is okay.

What inspired us

We didn't start Second Friend because mental health was a market opportunity. We started because we lost someone.

One of my football buddies was struggling with depression, but like many people going through difficult times, what he was experiencing wasn't always visible to those around him. Accessing professional counseling also felt financially out of reach. When we lost him to suicide, it left us with a question we couldn't ignore:

“What if asking for help had felt easier, more accessible, or simply possible at the right time?”

We'll never know whether one conversation could have changed what happened, and we will never claim that Second Friend can save everyone. But his story made us realize how many people struggle silently — sometimes because they cannot afford support, sometimes because they fear judgment, and sometimes because taking that first step feels harder than pretending everything is okay.

Second Friend was born from that realization: to make it easier for students to reach a real human and start the conversation when they need it.

We can't change what happened. But we can try to build a world where fewer people feel they have to face everything alone.

Questions students ask us

Why is Second Friend better than other mental health apps?

Because you talk to a real human being, every single time. Most apps today quietly hand your struggles to a chatbot — a program that has never felt anxiety before an exam or homesickness at midnight. Our counselors have lived through real life and trained for years to help you through yours. You'll never be told "As an AI language model…" when you're at your lowest.

Why NO AI? Isn't AI available 24/7?

AI can imitate empathy, but it cannot feel it. Studies show AI chatbots sometimes give harmful advice in crisis situations, and they can't notice what a human notices — hesitation in your voice, silence between messages, the thing you're not saying. A real counselor earns your trust, remembers your story, and is accountable for your care. That's something no algorithm can offer. We'd rather be human than scalable.

Is it confidential? Will my college find out?

No. Your sessions are completely private. Your college, professors, and parents are never notified that you use Second Friend. Nothing appears on your academic record.

Who are the counselors?

Verified professionals — psychologists and counselors who are background-checked, qualification-verified, and experienced specifically with college-age concerns like academic stress, relationships, anxiety, and identity.

What if I'm in serious crisis right now?

If you're thinking about harming yourself, please reach out immediately to the Government of India's national mental health helpline Tele-MANAS: 14416 (available 24/7, free, in multiple languages). Second Friend is support for everyday struggles — for emergencies, always call Tele-MANAS first.

How much does it cost?

We're launching with colleges as partners, so many students will get access free through their institution. Individual plans will be affordable and announced at launch. Joining the waitlist is completely free.
